RizzGuru Child Safety Standards

Standards against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Last updated: September 3, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

RizzGuru is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and has zero tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation of children, or any other conduct that endangers children.

RizzGuru does not permit the creation, upload, sharing, distribution, solicitation, promotion, or facilitation of CSAM or any other content or behavior that sexually exploits or endangers children.

Zero-tolerance policy: Any content or behavior involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child is strictly prohibited on RizzGuru.

2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

Users must not use RizzGuru to create, upload, request, distribute, promote, or facilitate:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of children.
  • Grooming or attempts to sexually exploit a child.
  • Sexual solicitation involving a minor.
  • Sextortion or coercion involving a child.
  • Trafficking or sexual exploitation of children.
  • Sexualized depictions of minors.
  • Attempts to obtain sexual images or information from minors.
  • Any other content or behavior that facilitates child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Attempts to circumvent these rules, including through coded language, links, images, or other methods, are also prohibited.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

RizzGuru provides users with an in-app mechanism to report child-safety concerns and potentially harmful content or behavior.

Users should report suspected CSAE, CSAM, grooming, exploitation, or any other child-safety concern as soon as possible using the reporting functionality available within the app.

Reports should contain enough information to help us identify and review the reported account, content, or behavior. Users should not download, save, or redistribute suspected CSAM.

4. Handling of Reports

RizzGuru takes reports of child sexual abuse and exploitation seriously. When we become aware of suspected CSAE or CSAM, we may take appropriate enforcement action consistent with our policies and applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

  • Reviewing the reported content or account.
  • Removing prohibited content.
  • Restricting or disabling accounts involved in violations.
  • Preserving relevant information where legally appropriate.
  • Escalating serious child-safety matters for appropriate action.
  • Reporting confirmed CSAM or other legally reportable activity to the appropriate authorities or reporting organizations when required by applicable law.

5. Cooperation With Authorities

RizzGuru complies with applicable child-safety laws and regulations. Where legally required, we cooperate with appropriate law enforcement, governmental authorities, and recognized child-safety organizations concerning reports of child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Where applicable, confirmed CSAM may be reported to the relevant national or regional authority in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

7. Enforcement

Users who violate these Child Safety Standards may have their content removed and their access to RizzGuru restricted or terminated.

RizzGuru may take additional measures when necessary to protect users and comply with applicable laws and platform requirements.

8. Prevention and Safety

RizzGuru is committed to maintaining safeguards designed to reduce the risk of child sexual abuse and exploitation on the platform. We encourage users to report suspicious behavior rather than engaging with or redistributing potentially harmful material.

Child safety is an ongoing responsibility, and these standards may be updated when necessary to improve our safety practices and maintain compliance with applicable requirements.

9. Relationship to Other Policies

These Child Safety Standards supplement RizzGuru's other applicable policies, including its Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and community or user-content rules.

In the event of a conflict concerning child safety, the stricter safety requirement will apply to the extent permitted by applicable law.
