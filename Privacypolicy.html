Depending on how you use RizzGuru, you may provide text, messages, screenshots, prompts, or other content to generate or improve replies.

  • Generate suggested replies
  • Provide the application's features

We may automatically collect certain technical and usage information, such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Crash and diagnostic information
